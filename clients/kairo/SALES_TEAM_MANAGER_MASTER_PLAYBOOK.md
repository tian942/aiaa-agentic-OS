# Kairo Sales Team Manager — Master Playbook

This is the one doc you need. Everything else is linked from here — you should never need to go hunting for a link.

---

## Your Job, In One Paragraph

You run the cold-calling floor: hire callers, get them live fast, keep them producing, fire the ones who don't. Everything in this doc is just the "how." You are accountable for exactly **two numbers** — nothing else:

| KPI | Target |
|---|---|
| **Caller Activation Speed** | Under 70 hours from first reach-out to a caller being live and dialing (2 calls minimum in that window — see the Onboarding Pipeline below) |
| **Revenue per Caller** | Minimum $750/week per caller |

Our biggest current problem is **retention and slow starts** — callers who take too long to get going, or who drop off before they ever produce. Everything below exists to fix that. If a caller isn't live within 70 hours of saying yes, or isn't hitting $750/week, that's the thing to fix first — before anything else on this page.

## Who We Are & What We Do

**Kairo** is a growth firm. We fill calendars with qualified meetings so clients get more customers without extra work on their end. Cold calling is our core service — here's the full scope of what that includes:

- Cold calling setup and infrastructure
- Script development (hooks, qualification, objection handling, CTA)
- List building and targeted lead sourcing
- Hiring, training, and managing dedicated callers
- Daily campaign management and quality assurance
- Direct setting on calendar
- Call tracking and performance reporting
- CRM setup + management for lead flow
- All tracking + pipeline integration

You are the person who makes the "hiring, training, and managing dedicated callers" line actually happen every day.

## The Onboarding Pipeline — Candidate to Live Caller

This is the sequence that determines whether you hit the 70-hour KPI. Five steps, in order.

### Step 1 — Hiring (source of truth)

All applicants live in the **[Hiring Sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1X9O9Hw-zV2Q0vtnnKPOOeEGalGNauznxipKdVrSDlKw/edit)**. Each applicant gets a Score and a Status. Status moves through:

`REACHED OUT` → `RAMP UP` → `ACTIVE` (or → `Left` if they drop out or you cut them)

The "On Offer" column shows which client they're assigned to (Kairo / PreCog / Abcns / Alvearium Cyber). This sheet is also your roster — it's where you see everyone's current stage at a glance.

### Step 2 — WhatsApp Reach-Out (send in this order)

**Message 1 — First contact, right after they're selected:**
> Hey [name]!
>
> Tian here, from Kairo. Really glad to have you on the team, your application stood out to us.
>
> Just getting in touch so you've always got my direct contact and don't need to bounce through anyone else if you need something.
>
> shoot me a call as soon as you can so I know this is the right number, and we can answer your questions and get you started!

**This first call happening is your clock starting.** Get it same day.

**Message 2 — After the intro call, send onboarding materials:**
> Great meeting you today, and congratulations on being selected to join the Kairo team again.
>
> Before we get you started, we'd like you to spend around 15 to 20 minutes completing our onboarding. It begins with a short Welcome Email (around 2 minutes) - please check your email, followed by two short videos that explain who we are, how we operate, and the opportunities available to you within the company.
>
> Once you've completed everything, let me know and we'll get you set up and ready to start making calls.
> Welcome aboard. We're excited to have you on the team.

**Message 3 — Once they confirm materials are done, send QUO + pre-dial checklist:**
> Hey [NAME]
>
> I just invited you to QUO. Please check your email and confirm that you're in. Super excited to get you started with Kairo!
>
> Before you begin, please confirm the following:
>
> You have accepted and saved the Team Huddle calendar invite for Monday, Wednesday, and Friday at 4:00 PM CET in your Google Calendar.
> You are familiar with QUO.
> You know how to submit the EOD report and EOD numbers.
> You know how to create a Post call handoff document after you set a call.
>
> Once you've confirmed these steps, you can start dialing.
>
> I'll be available during the calls if you have any questions.
>
> Super hyped to have you back. Let's get it going!

**This second call/confirmation is call #2.** Once they confirm all 4 checklist items, they're live. That's the finish line for the 70-hour clock.

### Step 3 — Interview Format

No group interviews. **One-on-one WhatsApp calls, during the day, same day if possible.** The reason: the people worth hiring go fast — if you're slow to respond, they're gone. Speed beats process here.

### Step 4 — Onboarding Materials

Welcome email + two short videos (who we are, how we operate, the opportunity) — sent automatically as part of Message 2 above. The caller confirms completion before you send Message 3.

### Step 5 — Assign, Warm Up, Go Live

Once confirmed, assign them to a client and add them to that client's tracker (see Client Directory below). New numbers need to warm up before full volume — this is the fixed ramp:

| Day | Max Dials |
|---|---|
| 1 | 30 |
| 2 | 45 |
| 3 | 60 |
| 4 | 60 |
| 5–7 | 50 |
| 8+ | +7/day, no cap |

Before they dial anything, confirm they personally have: (a) access to their lead list, (b) access to their tracker, (c) know how to file the EOD report by email, (d) know how to file the EOD report on the sheet, (e) understand the warmup process. All five, or they don't start.

## Day-to-Day Management

This is your daily loop, every working day:

1. **Check every caller's tracker.** For each client, open their tracker and check daily dials against their ramp stage or standard minimum (100/day once fully ramped). Anyone missing days or under-dialing gets flagged same day, not at week's end.
2. **Chase anyone who hasn't done their agreed reach-outs.** Don't wait for them to disappear — message them the same day activity drops. Slow response from you = they go quiet permanently. This is the retention leak.
3. **Run the Team Huddle.** 10–15 minutes, Monday/Wednesday/Friday at 4:00 PM CET. Cover: blockers, wins, anyone who needs a check-in.
4. **Check EOD reports daily.** Every caller submits an EOD report via the [EOD Report Form](https://tally.so/r/Y5XvbB) every working day, and logs the same numbers into their client's tracker (under the "Cold calls" tab). If someone's EOD is missing, that's your first flag that something's wrong — follow up same day.

## Caller-Level Standards (what you're enforcing, not your own KPI)

These are the standards every caller is held to. Full detail lives in each client's SOP doc — this is the summary you check against daily.

| Metric | Minimum | Target | Great |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dials/day | 100 | 150 | 200+ |
| Contact rate | 10% | 20% | 30%+ |
| Booking rate (of contacts) | 5% | 10% | 15%+ |
| Booked calls/day | 1 | 2 | 3+ |
| Show rate of booked calls | — | 70% | 85%+ |

**If a caller misses minimums:** first miss = flagged, conversation same day. Three misses in a week = formal warning. Repeated pattern = off the team, no exceptions. Hire and fire fast — this team runs on speed in both directions.
