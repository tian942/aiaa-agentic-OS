02 / Behind the movement

Sara
Tovšak.

Dancer · Coach · Performer

Sara Tovšak is a professional dancer, coach and performer. Her journey began with ballet at three, continued through jazz and contemporary dance, and led her to her greatest passion: pole dance and aerial hoop.

Her style brings together elegance, strength, femininity and expression. With years of stage experience, she creates performances that invite audiences to feel the energy, story and emotion in every movement.

As a coach, Sara helps dancers discover their strength, build confidence and find their own way of expressing themselves through movement.
